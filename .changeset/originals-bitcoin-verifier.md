Anchored Originals, verifier side (plan 011 PR 9a; plan 012). Asset states may list one did:btco alias: the anchoring entry, after which the sat never changes. `verifyOriginal` takes Bitcoin evidence (`SatEvidence`) and assembles the log from the anchoring sat: the anchoring inscription carries the whole log through the anchoring entry and every later one a single entry (P9.17), so `log` is optional with `bitcoin` and a copy is only compared; placement comes from each index's report, compared across indexes (P9.10, P9.11); the anchor, conflicting anchors and changes of anchor (P9.16), accepted, ignored and disputed inscriptions, author checks at inscription time (P9.15), and a provisional ownership report naming the indexes used. After anchoring no did:webvh alias may be added, replaced or re-added. The SDK adds `anchor`, a reorg-aware `InscriptionClock` (batched header reads, a `ClockStore` for restarts with `fileClockStore` in `@originals/sdk/originals/fs`, request budgets and cancellation), `satEvidence` (one read per named `index`), stores that retain the reported anchor, and Bitcoin evidence for `get` and `load`; the sat reader records block timestamps. The sat reader no longer fails on an inscription the sat's holder can add: a delegated or encoded one is reported observed but unsupported, a body past the byte budget observed but unread (`SatInscription.unsupported`; the verifier ignores or rejects it with a reason, P9.12, P9.14), and its request budget scales with the inscriptions listed (`SatSnapshotBudget.maxListedInscriptions`, `maxContentBytes`; past `maxInscriptions` bodies, later inscriptions are observed but unread); its errors carry a section 6 category. `InscriptionClock` refuses a block it holds once the chain has orphaned it, re-reads the parent of a block given without `prev`, and requires `prev` from a batched source (`BLOCK_PARENT_REQUIRED`).
