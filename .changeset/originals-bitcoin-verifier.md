Anchored Originals, verifier side (plan 011 PR 9a; plan 012). Asset states may list one did:btco alias: the anchoring entry, after which the sat never changes. `verifyOriginal` takes Bitcoin evidence (`SatEvidence`) and assembles the log from the anchoring sat: the anchoring inscription carries the whole log through the anchoring entry and every later one a single entry (P9.17), so `log` is optional with `bitcoin` and a copy is only compared; placement comes from each index's report, compared across indexes (P9.10, P9.11); the anchor, conflicting anchors and changes of anchor (P9.16), accepted, ignored and disputed inscriptions, author checks at inscription time (P9.15), and a provisional ownership report naming the indexes used. After anchoring no did:webvh alias may be added, replaced or re-added. The SDK adds `anchor`, a reorg-aware `InscriptionClock` (batched header reads, a `ClockStore` for restarts with `fileClockStore` in `@originals/sdk/originals/fs`, request budgets and cancellation), `satEvidence` (one read per named `index`), stores that retain the reported anchor, and Bitcoin evidence for `get` and `load`; the sat reader records block timestamps.
