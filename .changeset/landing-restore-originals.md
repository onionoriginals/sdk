A signed-in author sees "Your Originals": the ones this browser keeps, and those published from another device. Publishing records the Original's alias in the account index (`POST /api/originals` takes an optional `alias`, which must be in the caller's own namespace, must be named for that Original, and never changes once recorded). `DemoEngine.restore(did)` fetches the host's accepted log on a client that doesn't hold it, verifies it, reads it back as a stranger, and continues editing from the host's head with the author's own signer. If the host moves on during a restore, it follows a head that descends; a failed read-back leaves the page unchanged and the copy resumable. Publication is complete on the stranger's read-back alone: recording the Original in the account index is a separate step, retried on open and from the page, and reported as `accountIndex: "listed" | "not-listed"`, so a full index (507) never leaves a verified Original unpublished (#976 B, G3, G5).
