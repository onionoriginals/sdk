did:btco resolution follows the did:btco method v0.2.0 (plan 011 PR 9c; owner decision Q15). A did:btco's DID document is its inscription's CBOR metadata, versions follow chain order and deactivation is a `deactivated: true` document; the earlier content-based convention (document as inscription content, a 🔥 marker) is no longer read. `DIDManager` resolves did:btco only through an `ordinalsProvider` with `getSatSnapshot` (RegtestProvider, QuickNodeProvider, OrdMockProvider); a `bitcoinRpcUrl` alone now refuses with `ORD_PROVIDER_REQUIRED`. New `btcoDidInscription` and `btcoDidDeactivationInscription` build v0.2.0 inscriptions. `mode: 'current'` reads a did:btco again, a read whose chain tip moved is unresolvable, and a read of another network fails with `BTCO_NETWORK_MISMATCH`. The internal content-fetch adapters are removed. The resolver uses the Originals verifier's strict did:btco grammar (`parseBtcoDid` agrees with `parseBtcoAlias`), builds the verifier's `SatEvidence` through `satEvidence` (each inscription placed by the sat and network the index reports, the index named by the new `index` option), so an inscription the index reports on another sat or network is not a version, and a placement reported inconsistently is `unresolvable` rather than a stale latest version. `satEvidence` accepts any `clock` with `at`. A delegated or encoded inscription on the DID's sat is not a version and does not fail resolution; an inscription the sat reader did not read (past its byte budget) makes resolution `unresolvable`, since it could be a newer version.
