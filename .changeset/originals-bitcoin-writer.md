Writing anchored Originals to Bitcoin (plan 011 PR 10; plan 012), in the new `@originals/sdk/originals/bitcoin` entry. `prepareEntryInscription` inscribes an Original's head entry on its anchoring sat through the sat inscription builder (the first funding UTXO must hold the sat, so only its holder can extend the Original, and the head must be the next entry Bitcoin can accept); `submitPreparedInscriptionOnSat` and `resumeInscriptionOnSat` give durable submission and same-pair retries; `readAnchored` reads the sat back, refusing a read of another network, so the published boundary is what Bitcoin shows, never pending intent. Committed entries Bitcoin does not show yet are caught up in order: `nextPendingEntry` compares the committed local head with the accepted Bitcoin head and lists the entries between them; `prepareEntryInscription` prepares the next of them by default, inscribing the stored signed line verbatim (an explicit `versionId` that is not next is `HEAD_NOT_NEXT`, and a caught-up Original is `NOTHING_TO_INSCRIBE`); and `catchUp` re-reads Bitcoin and the recorded pairs to return one step (`prepare`, `resume`, `wait-for-confirmation` or `done`), so a pending publication is resumed or waited on rather than prepared twice, an entry Bitcoin already accepted is never inscribed again, and each accepted entry lands in a later block. Verified end to end on Bitcoin Core 31.1 and ord 0.29.0 regtest.
