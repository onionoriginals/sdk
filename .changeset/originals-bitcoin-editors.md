Anchored editor entries (Originals spec P5.17, P5.18, P9.12, P9.15). An editor entry inscribed on the anchoring sat is checked against the editor DID's state at its inscription time, as an author entry is against the author DID's (versionTime with the named-later-version exception for did:vh and did:webvh, chain order for did:btco), and is ignored with `author-deactivated-at-inscription-time` once the author DID's state at its inscription time is deactivated. Editor entries inscribed by the sat's holder are accepted; only author entries anchor; chain order on the sat decides between an editor entry and the revocation of its grant. A disputed inscription that no index reports as an Originals inscription no longer holds back later inscriptions on the anchoring sat (`readSat`'s `couldCount`; a disputed Originals inscription still does, P9.10), and an unverified anchoring reports the copy's head before its anchoring entry when Bitcoin evidence was supplied. A did:btco history stops, with missing evidence, at an inscription whose metadata the reader did not read, never skipping it; a delegated or encoded inscription's own metadata is read (`satEvidence` passes it on; absent means not read) and is a version candidate (P5.13, PROVISIONAL).
