Publishing resumes after an interruption and is complete only once a stranger can verify it. The engine keeps a publication record (alias built, uploaded, complete) in the account's store, so a publication interrupted before its entry, after it, or during the uploads resumes after a browser restart under the same alias and checkpoint, with no second publish entry. `publish()` resolves only after a cookie-less reader verifies the exact hosted snapshot with every byte; otherwise it throws `PublicationNotVerifiedError`. Until then the Original isn't shown as published (`publication: "incomplete"`, layer `did:vh`) and edits wait. A publish cancelled before its entry publishes nothing: the Original stays editable and shows no web address, and the next publish reuses the alias. A client whose read-back finds the host at a later head that verifiably descends from its own checkpoint syncs to it and completes; a head that diverges stays a conflict (#976 B, G4, G5).
