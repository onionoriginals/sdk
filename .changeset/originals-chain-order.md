Chain order decides after an unverifiable inscription (P9.15, P9.13; originals-spec #51, merged at master `19d3e82a0`). While an inscription extending the accepted head is unverifiable, a later publication extending that head, or extending the unverifiable one, is ignored as `waiting-for-earlier-unverifiable` with `waitingOn`; once the earlier one verifies it is accepted, or rejected and the next considered. BREAKING: `anchoring.headChange` is `{ previous, current, verified, resolved, unverified }` and reports any move of the head caused by an inscription's verifiability, either way (reasons `unverifiable-inscription-resolved`, `accepted-inscription-unverifiable`, besides `unverifiable-inscription-accepted`). An inscription whose signer version is unverifiable but which fails any check not depending on it is rejected (`VerifyOptions.unauthorized` lets a profile run those checks); so is an entry naming a did:vh or did:webvh version past the 1,000-version limit or after a deactivated version (`AUTHOR_VERSION_IMPOSSIBLE`), and an entry in the anchoring inscription naming a version its signer's history shows is not that DID's (`AUTHOR_VERSION_NOT_IN_HISTORY`). A did:btco read gap is missing evidence only for entries that depend on it (P5.13). `report.notices` (`unverifiable-inscriptions`, `publications-waiting`) accompanies a current result (section 7); the writer's `wait-for-evidence` carries `waitingOn` for its own waiting pair, and the landing's anchoring status and panel show the notices and what they list.
