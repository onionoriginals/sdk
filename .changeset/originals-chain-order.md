Chain order decides after an unverifiable inscription (P9.15, P9.13; originals-spec #51, merged at master `19d3e82a0`). While an inscription extending the accepted head is unverifiable, a later publication extending that head, or any waiting publication, directly or through others, is ignored as `waiting-for-earlier-unverifiable` with `waitingOn`; each candidate is judged in full first, so one that would be rejected on its own is rejected, and one unverifiable itself is listed in `unverifiable` as well. Once the earlier one verifies it is accepted, or rejected and the next considered. BREAKING: `anchoring.headChange` is `{ previous, current, verified, resolved, unverified, vanished }` and reports any move of the head caused by an inscription's verifiability or a reorganization; the result stays current and carries the notice `head-changed` (a reorganization that drops the retained head is still `authoritative-source-changed`). `RetainedAnchor` keeps `unverifiable` and `accepted` inscription ids. An inscription whose signer version is unverifiable but which fails any check not depending on it is rejected (`VerifyOptions.unauthorized` lets a profile run those checks); a did:vh or did:webvh versionId that is malformed, numbered below 1, past the 1,000-version limit or after a deactivated version is `AUTHOR_VERSION_IMPOSSIBLE`, with or without the signer's history and in the anchoring inscription too, where a version held under another versionId is `AUTHOR_VERSION_NOT_IN_HISTORY`. A did:btco read gap is missing evidence only for entries that depend on it (P5.13). `report.notices` (`head-changed`, `unverifiable-inscriptions`, `publications-waiting`) accompanies a current result (section 7): the CLI's verdict line (`verdictLine`, `noticeLines`) and the landing's anchoring status and panel name each listed inscription, and the writer's `wait-for-evidence` carries `waitingOn` for its own waiting pair.
