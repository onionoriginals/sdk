Hosted publication on the Originals Protocol, behind `hostedPublication` (off). A signed-in author publishes an Original under a did:webvh alias in their own namespace, controlled by their Turnkey identity key; the host accepts only the hosted layout, checks each resource against the Original's signed asset log, and serves it with its signed media type. The CEL-era host key shapes are gone.
